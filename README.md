> The 20-minute window right after sunset when cool cobalt ambient sky meets warm incandescent streetlights.

## 4. The 5 Rules for Hyperrealistic AI Photography

To prevent AI diffusion engines from producing waxy, cartoonish plastic results:

1. **Optical Camera Constraints:** Always specify real hardware (e.g., `Sony A7R V`, `Hasselblad H6D-100c`, `Leica M11`, or `iPhone 15 Pro rear camera candid`).
2. **Focal Length & Aperture:** Use `85mm f/1.4` for flattering facial compression or `35mm f/1.8` for environmental storytelling.
3. **Dual Color Temperature Lighting:** Contrast cool ambient fill (`6500K`) against warm tungsten keys (`2800K`).
4. **Micro-Texture Descriptors:** Include `subtle skin pores`, `perspiration sheen`, `chalk dust smudges`, and `organic fabric slubs`.
5. **Anti-Airbrushing Exclusions:** Negate `porcelain smoothing`, `3D digital render`, `CGI video game character`, and `plastic wax skin`.
